# 来源与许可 · Sources and licensing

已有来源和许可随文件保留；个人维护与迭代不替代上游作者署名。未为没有许可声明的部分添加统一授权，第三方资料按其原有许可使用。

Existing source notices and licenses are retained. Maintenance and iteration do not replace upstream authorship. No blanket license is added to material without an existing license; third-party material retains its own terms.
