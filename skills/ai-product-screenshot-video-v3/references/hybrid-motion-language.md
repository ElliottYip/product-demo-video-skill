# Hybrid motion language

## Purpose

Use energetic editorial motion to make static official screenshots feel native to information-feed video. Motion explains hierarchy; screenshots carry factual proof.

## Core sequence

1. Hook, 0–3 seconds: large thesis, fast character reveal, concise consequence, and one immediately recognizable proof frame.
2. Proof loop, roughly 4–8 seconds per idea: ordinal/badge, title, official context screenshot, exact static focus crop, two to four feature cards, and one takeaway.
3. Caption layer: phrase-level text follows the actual master audio; only important terms change color.
4. CTA tail, three seconds: simplified background with a generic search or follow action. Do not imitate a platform interface.

## Static proof rule

The following elements must never appear in any animation timeline:

- screenshot `<img>` nodes;
- context and focus crop containers;
- evidence borders;
- proof labels;
- screenshot masks or clipping paths.

Screenshot variation comes from editorial cuts between separately prepared crops, not transforms within a scene. All crop coordinates must originate from multimodal evidence review.

## Allowed motion

- title character reveal;
- ordinal and trust badge entrance;
- staggered feature cards;
- takeaway entrance;
- bottom caption entrance and term color emphasis;
- low-amplitude decorative particles;
- hard cuts or short background-only transitions.

Avoid flashing boxes, pulsing evidence borders, fake cursors, needless arrows, rapid zooms, and overlapping entrance animations. Keep important text visible long enough to read.

## Reference-video adaptation

Extract abstract properties only: information density, hierarchy, approximate phase lengths, type scale, entrance rhythm, palette family, and CTA role. Do not reproduce identifiable wording, watermark, account ID, music, logo, exact coordinates, or distinctive full-frame composition.
